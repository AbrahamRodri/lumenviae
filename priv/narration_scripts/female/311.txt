With the servants the Lord, with the criminals the Judge, cometh to be baptized. [thoughtful] But be not thou troubled; for in these humiliations His exaltation doth most shine forth. [long pause]

[gently] [reverently] For He who vouchsafed to be borne so long in a Virgin's womb, and to come forth thence with our nature, and to be smitten with rods, and crucified, and to suffer all the rest which He suffered;—why marvellest thou if He vouchsafed also to be baptized, and to come with the rest to His servant. [pause] For the amazement lay in that one thing, that being God, He would be made Man; but the rest after this all follows in course of reason.
