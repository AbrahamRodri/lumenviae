Let us contemplate in this mystery,
how Our Lord Jesus Christ was most cruelly scourged in Pilate's house, the number of stripes they gave him being about five thousand. [long pause]

[softly] [reverently] O Mother of God, overflowing fountain of patience, through those stripes thy only and much beloved Son vouchsafed to suffer for us, [short pause] obtain of him for us grace to mortify our rebellious senses, to avoid the occasion of sin, and to be ready to suffer everything rather than offend God. [long pause]

Amen.
