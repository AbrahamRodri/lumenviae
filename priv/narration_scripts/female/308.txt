We have heard the Gospel, and in it the Lord reproving those who knew how to discern the face of the sky, and know not how to discover the time of faith, the kingdom of heaven which is at hand. [thoughtful] Now this He said to the Jews; but His words reach even unto us. [long pause]

Now the Lord Jesus Christ Himself began the preaching of His Gospel in this way; "Repent ye, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand." [short pause] In like manner too John the Baptist and His forerunner began thus; "Repent ye, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand." [long pause]

And now the Lord rebuketh those who would not repent, when "the kingdom of heaven was at hand." "The kingdom of heaven," as He saith Himself, "will not come with observation." [short pause] And again He saith, "The kingdom of heaven is within you." [pause] [softly with quiet resolve] Let every one then wisely receive the admonitions of the Master, that he may not lose the season of the mercy of the Saviour, which is now being dealt out, as long as the human race is spared. For to this end is man spared, that he may be converted, and that he may not be to be condemned. [short pause] God only knoweth when the end of the world shall come: nevertheless now is the time of faith.
