And now they laid the cross upon Him as a malefactor. [thoughtful] For even the wood they abominated, and endured not even to touch it. [short pause] This was also the case in the type; for Isaac bare the wood. [pause] But then the matter stopped at the will of his father, for it was the type; while here it proceeded to action, for it was the reality. [long pause]

"And He came to the place of a skull." [short pause] Some say that Adam died there, and there lieth; and that Jesus in this place where death had reigned, there also set up the trophy. [pause] [gently] [reverently] For He went forth bearing the Cross as a trophy over the tyranny of death: and as conquerors do, so He bare upon His shoulders the symbol of victory.
