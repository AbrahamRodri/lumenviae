First: As Our Lady visited Elizabeth, St. John Baptist, being in his mother's womb, felt the visitation which Our Lady made. [thoughtful] "And when Elizabeth heard the salutation of Our Lady, the infant leaped in her womb. And Elizabeth, full of the Holy Ghost, cried out with a loud voice, and said: 'Blessed be thou among women and blessed be the fruit of thy womb!'" [long pause]

[softly] [with quiet wonder] Second: Our Lady sings the canticle, saying: "'My soul doth magnify the Lord!'" [long pause]

Third: "Mary abode with Elizabeth about three months: and then she returned to her house."
