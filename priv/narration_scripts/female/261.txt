First: The Angel St. Gabriel, saluting Our Lady, announced to her the Conception of Christ our Lord. [short pause] [with quiet wonder] "The Angel entering where Mary was, saluted her saying: 'Hail full of grace. Thou shalt conceive in thy womb and shalt bring forth a son.'" [long pause]

Second: The Angel confirms what he said to Our Lady, telling of the conception of St. John Baptist, saying to her: [short pause] "'And behold thy cousin Elizabeth hath conceived a son in her old age.'" [long pause]

[gently] [reverently] Third: Our Lady answered the Angel: [short pause] "'Behold the handmaid of the Lord: be it done to me according to thy word!'"
