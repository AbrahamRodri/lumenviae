As Mary uttered the words: "May it be done unto me according to thy word!" I saw an apparition of the Holy Ghost. [with quiet wonder] The countenance was human and the whole apparition environed by dazzling splendor, as if surrounded by wings. [long pause]

From the breast and hands, I saw issuing three streams of light. They penetrated the right side of the Blessed Virgin and united into one under her heart. [short pause] At that instant Mary became perfectly transparent and luminous. [pause] It was as if opacity disappeared like darkness before that flood of light.
