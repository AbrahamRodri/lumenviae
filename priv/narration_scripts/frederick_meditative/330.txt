The heavenly Lady, absorbed in joy, approached her most loving Son and in the presence of the whole assembly, spoke to Him the words recorded by saint Luke: "Son, why hast Thou done so to us? Behold thy father and I have sought Thee sorrowing". [tenderly] This loving complaint the heavenly Mother uttered with equal reverence and affection, adoring Him as God and manifesting her maternal affliction. [long pause]

[gently] [reverently] The Lord answered: "Why is it that you sought Me? Did you not know that I must be about my Father's business?" [long pause]

[softly] [thoughtful] The Evangelist says that they did not understand the mystery of these words; for it was hidden at the time to most holy Mary and saint Joseph. [short pause] And for two reasons; on the one hand, the interior joy of now reaping what they had sown in so much sorrow, and the visible presence of their precious Treasure, entirely filled the faculties of their souls; [short pause] and on the other hand, the time for the full comprehension of what had just been treated of in this discussion had not yet arrived for them.
