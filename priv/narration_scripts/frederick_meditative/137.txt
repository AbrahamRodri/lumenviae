Let us contemplate in this mystery,
how the Blessed Virgin Mary, understanding from the angel that her cousin St. Elizabeth had conceived, went with haste into the mountains of Judea to visit her, bearing her divine Son within her womb, and remained with her three months. [long pause]

[gently] [reverently] O holy Virgin, most spotless mirror of humility, by that exceeding charity which moved thee to visit thy cousin St. Elizabeth, obtain for us, through thine intercession, that our hearts being visited by thy divine Son, and freed from all sin, we may praise and give thanks to him forever. [long pause]

Amen.
