The night was dark and tranquil over the little town of Nazareth, when Joseph went forth. [thoughtful] No commandment of God ever found such promptitude in highest saint or readiest angel, as this one had found in Mary. [short pause] [gently] [tenderly] She heard Joseph’s words, and she smiled on him in silence as he spoke. [long pause]

There was no perturbation, no hurry, although there was all a mother’s fear. [short pause] She took up her treasure, as He slept, and went forth with Joseph into the cold starlight; for poverty has few preparations to make. [pause] She was leaving home again. [softly] [sad] Terror and hardship, the wilderness and heathendom were before her; and she confronted all with the calm anguish of an already broken heart.
