Mary's words to Jesus had been uttered in a low tone, but Jesus' reply, as well as His command to draw water, was given in a loud voice. [thoughtful] When the jugs filled with water had been placed, six in number, on the side table, Jesus went and blessed them. [short pause] As He retook His place at table, He called to a servant: "Draw off now, and bring a drink to the steward!" [long pause]

[softly] [with quiet wonder] When this latter had tasted the wine, he approached the bridegroom and said: "Every man at first setteth forth good wine, and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse. But thou hast kept the good wine until now." [pause] He did not know that the wine was provided by Jesus as was also this whole course of the feast. [short pause] That was a secret between the Holy Family and the family of the bridal pair.
