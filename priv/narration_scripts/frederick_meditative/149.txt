Let us contemplate in this mystery,
how the glorious Virgin, twelve years after the resurrection of her Son, passed out of this world unto him, and was by him assumed into heaven, accompanied by the holy angels. [long pause]

[reverently] O most prudent Virgin, who, entering the heavenly palaces, 
didst fill the angels with joy and man with hope, [short pause] vouchsafe to intercede for us at the hour of our death, that, being delivered from the illusions and temptations of the devil, we may joyfully and securely pass out of this temporal state, to enjoy the happiness of eternal life. [long pause]

[softly] Amen.
