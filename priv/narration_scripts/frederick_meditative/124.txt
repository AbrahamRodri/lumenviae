Every child is an arrow shot out of the bow of its mother, but its target is God. [thoughtful] Children have come through mothers, but they do not belong to them. [short pause] Mary acknowledges this claim of divinity on her Child by presenting Him back again to God, as she offers the temple of His Body in the temple made by hands. [long pause]

[gently] [warmly] Mary here anticipated the joy of every mother who brings her child to the baptismal font, where God may claim His own. [short pause] But in the case of Mary, the Child was claimed for sacrifice, as the aged Simeon said that He was a sign to be contradicted, for the cross is the contradiction. [long pause]

[softly] [sad] Mary was even told that a sword her own soul would pierce. That would happen when her Son on the cross would have His heart pierced with a lance. [short pause] Through His Body and her soul would go that one stroke of the sword. [pause] She was the only mother who ever brought a life into the world to die. [long pause]

[softly with quiet resolve] It is not so much our presents that God wants from us, as it is our presence, as we offer our life to Him.
