For this cause Christ, to teach us that of mere goodness He had entered on this, so far from waiting for the sick to come to Him, of Himself hastened unto them, bearing them two of the greatest blessings; one, the gospel of the kingdom; another, the perfect cure of all their diseases. [thoughtful] And not a city did He overlook, not a village did He hasten by, but visited every place. [long pause]

[gently] [warmly] And not even at this doth He stop, but He exhibits also another instance of His forethought. [short pause] That is, "When He saw," it is said, "the multitudes, He was moved with compassion on them, because they were troubled, and scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd. [pause] Then saith He unto His disciples, The harvest truly is plenteous, but the laborers are few, pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that He will send forth laborers into His harvest." [long pause]

[softly] [reverently] See again His freedom from vainglory. [short pause] That He may not draw all men unto Himself, He sends out His disciples.
