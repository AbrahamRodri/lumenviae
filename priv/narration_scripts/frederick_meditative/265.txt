First: Christ our Lord, of the age of twelve years, went up from Nazareth to Jerusalem. [long pause]

[thoughtful] Second: Christ our Lord remained in Jerusalem, and His parents did not know it. [long pause]

[gently] [with quiet wonder] Third: The three days passed, they found Him disputing in the Temple, and seated in the midst of the doctors, and His parents asking Him where He had been, [short pause] He answered: "'Did you not know that it behooves Me to be in the things which are My Father's?'"
