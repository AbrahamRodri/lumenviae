She felt His eye, and she looked up into His face. [tenderly] Never did two such faces look into each other, and speak such unutterable love as this. [short pause] The Father held Mary up in His arms lest she should perish under the load of love, and the loud cry went out from the hilltop, hushing Mary’s soul into an agony of silence, [short pause] [softly] [sad] and the Head drooped towards her, and the eye closed, and the Soul passed her, like a flash, and sank into the earth,—and a wind arose, and stirred the mantle of darkness, and the sun cleared itself of the moon’s shadow, and the roofs of the city glimmered white, and the birds began to sing, but only as if they were half reassured, [short pause] and Mary stood beneath the Cross a childless Mother. [long pause]

The third hour was gone.
