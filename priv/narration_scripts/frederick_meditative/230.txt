I did not witness the purification ceremonies of the other mothers; but I had an interior conviction that all the children offered on that day would receive special grace, and that some of the martyred innocents were among them. [long pause]

[softly] [with quiet wonder] When the Most Holy Child Jesus was laid upon the altar in the basket cradle, an indescribable light filled the Temple. [short pause] I saw that God was in that light, and I saw the heavens open up as far as the Most Holy Trinity.
