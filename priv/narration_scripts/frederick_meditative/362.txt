Here let us pause, and offer our Saviour a reparation for the insult he here receives. [softly] [sad] He is put in competition with a murderer, and the murderer is preferred! [short pause] Pilate makes an attempt to save Jesus: but, on what terms! — he must be put on a footing with a vile wretch, and, even so, be worsted! [short pause] Those very lips that, a few days back, sang "Hosannah to the Son of David," now clamour for his Crucifixion! [short pause] The City Magistrate and Governor pronounces him innocent; and yet, he condemns him to be scourged, because he fears a disturbance! [long pause]

[sighs softly] Jesus is made over to the soldiers, to be scourged. [short pause] They rudely strip him of his garments, and tie him to the pillar, which is kept for this kind of torture. [short pause] Fiercely do they strike him; the blood flows down his sacred Body. [pause] [gently] [reverently] Let us adore this the second Bloodshedding of our Jesus, whereby he expiates for the sins we and the whole world have committed by the flesh.
