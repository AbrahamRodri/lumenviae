On the night following the burial took place the bodily Assumption of the Blessed Virgin into Heaven. [thoughtful] I saw on this night several of the Apostles and holy women in the little garden, praying and singing Psalms before the grotto. [short pause] I saw a broad pathway of light descend from Heaven and rest upon the tomb. [long pause]

[softly] [with quiet wonder] In it were circles of glory full of angels, in the midst of whom the resplendent soul of the Blessed Virgin came floating down. Before her went her Divine Son, the marks of His Wounds flashing with light. [short pause] In the innermost circle, that which surrounded the holy soul of Mary, the angels appeared like the faces of very young children; in the second circle, they were like those of children from six to eight years old; and in the outermost, like the faces of youths. I could clearly distinguish only the face, the rest of the figure consisting of perfectly transparent light. [pause] Encircling the head of the Blessed Virgin like a crown, was a choir of blessed spirits. [long pause]

[gently] [thoughtful] I know not what those present saw of all this. But I saw that some gazed up in amazement and adoration, while others cast themselves prostrate in fright upon the earth. [short pause] These apparitions, becoming more and more distinct as they approached nearer, floated over the grotto, and another pathway of light issued from it and arose to the heavenly Jerusalem. [pause] [gently] [reverently] The blessed soul of Mary, floating before Jesus, penetrated through the rock and into the tomb, out of which she again arose radiant with light in her glorified body and, escorted by the entire multitude of celestial spirits, returned in triumph to the heavenly Jerusalem.
