[hushed, solemn] The hour of our Lord was at last come; his death-struggle had commenced; a cold sweat overspread every limb. John stood at the foot of the Cross, and wiped the feet of Jesus with his scapular. Magdalen was crouched to the ground in a perfect frenzy of grief behind the Cross. [short pause] [tender] The Blessed Virgin stood between Jesus and the good thief, supported by Salome and Mary of Cleophas, with her eyes rivetted on the countenance of her dying Son. [long pause] [quiet, grave] Jesus then said: [low, spent] 'It is consummated;' [short pause] [measured] and, raising his head, cried out in a loud voice, [clear, resonant] 'Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit.' [pause] [awed] These words, which he uttered in a clear and thrilling tone, resounded through heaven and earth; [short pause] [softly] and a moment after, he bowed down his head and gave up the ghost. [long pause] [hushed] I saw his soul, under the appearance of a bright meteor, penetrate the earth at the foot of the Cross. John and the holy women fell prostrate on the ground. [long pause] [measured, building] The centurion Abenadar had kept his eyes steadfastly fixed on the disfigured countenance of our Lord, and was perfectly overwhelmed by all that had taken place. When our Lord pronounced his last words, before expiring, in a loud tone, the earth trembled, and the rock of Calvary burst asunder, forming a deep chasm between the Cross of our Lord and that of Gesmas. [short pause] [solemn, awed] The voice of God, that solemn and terrible voice, had re-echoed through the whole universe; it had broken the solemn silence which then pervaded all nature. [short pause] [quiet, grave] All was accomplished. The soul of our Lord had left his body: his last cry had filled every breast with terror. The convulsed earth had paid homage to its Creator: the sword of grief had pierced the hearts of those who loved him. [long pause] [measured, moved] This moment was the moment of grace for Abenadar; his horse trembled under him; his heart was touched; it was rent like the hard rock; he threw his lance to a distance, struck his breast, and cried out: [pause] [fervent, loud] 'Blessed be the Most High God, the God of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Jacob; indeed this Man was the Son of God!' [short pause] [calm, warm] His words convinced many among the soldiers, who followed his example, and were likewise converted.
