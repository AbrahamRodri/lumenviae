[solemn, slow] Having finished and established the work of Redemption in all its perfection, it was becoming that the incarnate Word, just as He came forth from the Father to enter mortal life, should enter into immortal life of the Father through death. [short pause] Therefore Christ our Savior added the last words uttered by Him: "Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit." [pause] The Lord spoke these words in a loud and strong voice, so that the bystanders heard them. In pronouncing them He raised his eyes to heaven, as one speaking with the eternal Father, and with the last accent He gave up his spirit and inclined his head. [pause] By the divine force of these words Lucifer with all his demons were hurled into the deepest caverns of hell, there they lay motionless. [long pause] [grave, hushed] The invincible Queen and Mistress of all virtues understood these mysteries beyond the understanding of all creatures, as She was the Mother of the Savior and the Coadjutrix of his Passion. In order that She might participate in it to the end, just as She had felt in her own body the other torments of her Son, She now, though remaining alive, felt and suffered the pangs and agony of his death. [short pause] She did not die in reality; but this was because God miraculously preserved her life, when according to the natural course death should have followed. [long pause] [quiet, reverent] This miraculous aid was more wonderful than all the other favors She received during the Passion. For this last pain was more intense and penetrating; and all that the martyrs and the men sentenced to death have suffered from the beginning of the world cannot equal what the blessed Mary suffered during the Passion.
