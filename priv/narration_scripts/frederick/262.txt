[warm, narrative] First: As Our Lady visited Elizabeth, St. John Baptist, being in his mother's womb, felt the visitation which Our Lady made. [short pause] "And when Elizabeth heard the salutation of Our Lady, the infant leaped in her womb. [short pause] [with wonder] And Elizabeth, full of the Holy Ghost, cried out with a loud voice, and said: [short pause] 'Blessed be thou among women, and blessed be the fruit of thy womb!'" [long pause] [joyful, gentle] Second: Our Lady sings the canticle, saying: [short pause] "'My soul doth magnify the Lord!'" [long pause] [calm, measured] Third: "Mary abode with Elizabeth about three months: [short pause] and then she returned to her house."
