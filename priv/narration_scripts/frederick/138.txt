[hushed, tender] Let us contemplate in this mystery, [short pause] how the Blessed Virgin Mary, when the time of her delivery was come, brought forth our Redeemer Jesus Christ at midnight, and laid him in a manger, because there was no room for him in the inns at Bethlehem. [long pause] [softly, with devotion] O most pure Mother of God, through thy virginal and most joyful delivery, whereby thou gavest to the world thy only Son, Our Saviour, we beseech thee obtain for us, through thine intercession, the grace to lead such pure and holy lives in this world, that we may become worthy to sing without ceasing the mercies of thy Son, and his benefits to us by thee. [short pause] [quietly] Amen.
