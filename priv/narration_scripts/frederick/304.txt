[hushed, measured] The sun had set and it was dark, but the Apostles had not remarked the fact, so entrancing were Jesus' words and bearing. He became brighter and brighter, and apparitions of angelic spirits hovered around Him. [short pause] Peter saw them, for he interrupted Jesus with the question: [wondering] "Master, what does this mean?" [short pause] Jesus answered: [gentle, calm] "They serve Me!" [short pause] [eager, overwhelmed] Peter, quite out of himself, stretched forth his hands, exclaiming: "Master, are we not here? We will serve Thee in all things!" [pause] [softly, with wonder] Jesus began again His instructions, and along with the angelic apparitions flowed alternate streams of delicious perfumes, of celestial delights and contentment over the Apostles. Jesus meantime continued to shine with ever-increasing splendor, until He became as if transparent. The circle around them was so lighted up in the darkness of night that each little plant could be distinguished on the green sod as if in clear daylight. [short pause] [hushed] The three Apostles were so penetrated, so ravished that, when the light reached a certain degree, they covered their heads, prostrated on the ground, and there remained lying. [long pause] [awed, slow] It was about twelve o'clock at night when I beheld this glory at its height. I saw a shining pathway reaching from Heaven to earth, and on it angelic spirits of different choirs, all in constant movement. [short pause] Some were small, but of perfect form; others were merely faces peeping forth from the glancing light; some were in priestly garb, while others looked like warriors. [short pause] [warm, reverent] Each had some special characteristic different from that of the others, and from each radiated some special refreshment, strength, delight, and light.
