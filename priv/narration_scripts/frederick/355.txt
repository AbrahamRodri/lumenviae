[hushed, solemn] "And Jesus, when He had cried with a loud voice, yielded up the Ghost." [long pause] [measured, reverent] This is what He said, "I have power to lay down my life, and I have power to take it again," and, "I lay it down of myself." [short pause] So for this cause He cried with the voice, that it might be shown that the act is done by power. [pause] Mark at any rate saith, that "Pilate marvelled if He were already dead:" and that the centurion for this cause above all believed, because He died with power. [long pause] [slow, awed] This cry rent the veil, and opened the tombs, and made the house desolate.
