[solemn, measured] Let us contemplate in this mystery, [short pause] how those cruel ministers of Satan platted a crown of sharp thorns and cruelly pressed it on the sacred head of Our Lord Jesus Christ. [long pause] [softly, pleading] O Mother of our eternal Prince, the King of glory, by those sharp thorns wherewith his sacred head was pierced, we beseech thee, obtain, through thy intercession, that we may be delivered from all motions of pride, and escape that shame which our sins deserve at the day of judgment. [short pause] [quietly] Amen.
