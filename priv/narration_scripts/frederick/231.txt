[measured, storytelling] Jesus had been teaching two hours, when Joseph and Mary entered the Temple. They inquired after their Child of the Levites whom they knew, and received for answer that He was with the doctors in the lecture hall. But as they were not at liberty to enter that hall, they sent one of the Levites in to call Jesus. [short pause] Jesus sent them word that He must first finish what He was then about. [short pause] [gently, with sorrow] Mary was very much troubled at His not obeying at once, for this was the first time He had given His parents to understand that He had other commands than theirs to fulfill. [long pause] [measured] He continued to teach for another hour, and then He left the hall and joined His parents in the porch of Israel, the women's porch, leaving His hearers confounded, confused, and enraged. [short pause] [quietly] Joseph was quite awed and astonished, but he kept a humble silence. [short pause] Mary, however, drawing near to Jesus, said, [tender, sorrowful] "Child, why hast Thou done this to us? Behold, Thy father and I have sought Thee sorrowing!" [pause] [grave, gentle] But Jesus answered gravely, "Why have you sought Me? Do you not know that I must be about My Father's business?" [long pause] [softly] But they did not understand.
