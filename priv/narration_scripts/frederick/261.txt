[reverent, measured] First: The Angel St. Gabriel, saluting Our Lady, announced to her the Conception of Christ our Lord. [short pause] [softly, with wonder] "The Angel entering where Mary was, saluted her saying: 'Hail, full of grace. [short pause] Thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and shalt bring forth a son.'" [long pause] [calm, measured] Second: The Angel confirms what he said to Our Lady, telling of the conception of St. John Baptist, saying to her: [short pause] [gently] "'And behold, thy cousin Elizabeth hath conceived a son in her old age.'" [long pause] [quiet, slow] Third: Our Lady answered the Angel: [pause] [softly, with reverence] "'Behold the handmaid of the Lord: [short pause] be it done to me according to thy word!'"
