[hushed, reverent] As Mary uttered the words: [short pause] [softly] "May it be done unto me according to thy word!" [short pause] [awed] I saw an apparition of the Holy Ghost. The countenance was human, and the whole apparition environed by dazzling splendor, as if surrounded by wings. [long pause] [measured, full of wonder] From the breast and hands, I saw issuing three streams of light. They penetrated the right side of the Blessed Virgin, and united into one under her heart. [short pause] [quietly] At that instant Mary became perfectly transparent and luminous. [short pause] It was as if opacity disappeared like darkness before that flood of light.
