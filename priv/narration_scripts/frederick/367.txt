[hushed, still] The night was dark and tranquil over the little town of Nazareth, when Joseph went forth. [short pause] [reverent] No commandment of God ever found such promptitude in highest saint or readiest angel, as this one had found in Mary. [short pause] [tender] She heard Joseph’s words, and she smiled on him in silence as he spoke. [long pause] [calm, gentle] There was no perturbation, no hurry, although there was all a mother’s fear. She took up her treasure, as He slept, and went forth with Joseph into the cold starlight; for poverty has few preparations to make. [short pause] [sorrowful] She was leaving home again. Terror and hardship, the wilderness and heathendom were before her; [short pause] [softly, steady] and she confronted all with the calm anguish of an already broken heart.
