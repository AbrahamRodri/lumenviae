[warm, storytelling] Mary's words to Jesus had been uttered in a low tone, but Jesus' reply, as well as His command to draw water, was given in a loud voice. When the jugs filled with water had been placed, six in number, on the side table, Jesus went and blessed them. [short pause] As He retook His place at table, He called to a servant: [clear, gentle authority] "Draw off now, and bring a drink to the steward!" [long pause] [warm, a little amused] When this latter had tasted the wine, he approached the bridegroom and said: "Every man at first setteth forth good wine, and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse. [short pause] But thou hast kept the good wine until now." [pause] [quietly, confiding] He did not know that the wine was provided by Jesus, as was also this whole course of the feast. [short pause] That was a secret between the Holy Family and the family of the bridal pair.
