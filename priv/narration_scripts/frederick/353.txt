[earnest, measured] But let us not merely read of these things, but bear them in our mind; [short pause] [slow, grave] the crown of thorns, the robe, the reed, the blows, the smiting on the cheek, the spittings, the irony. [pause] [gentle, instructive] These things, if continually meditated on, are sufficient to take down all anger; and if we be mocked at, if we suffer injustice, let us still say, "The servant is not greater than his Lord"; and let us bring forward the words of the Jews, which they uttered in their madness, saying, "Thou art a Samaritan, and hast a devil"; and, "He casteth out devils by Beelzebub." [long pause] [softly, with reverence] For on this account He bare all these things, in order that we might walk in His footsteps, and endure those mockings which disturb more than any other kind of reproach. [long pause] [tender, heartfelt] Yet nevertheless He not only bare these things, but even used every means to save and deliver from the appointed punishment those who did them. For He sent the Apostles also for their salvation, at least thou hearest them saying, that, "We know that through ignorance ye did it"; [short pause] and by these means drawing them to repentance.
