[calm, measured] While Jesus ascended from the depths of the baptismal well, Andrew and Saturnin, who were standing to the right of the Baptist around the triangular stone, threw about Him a large linen cloth with which He dried His Person. They then put on Him a long, white baptismal robe. [short pause] After this Jesus stepped on the red triangular stone which lay to the right of the descent into the well; Andrew and Saturnin each laid one hand upon His shoulder, while John rested his upon His head. [long pause] [quietly, building] This part of the ceremony over, they were just about mounting the steps when the Voice of God came over Jesus, who was still standing alone and in prayer upon the stone. [awed] There came from Heaven a great, rushing wind like thunder. All trembled and looked up. [short pause] A cloud of white light descended, and I saw over Jesus a winged figure of light, as if flowing over Him like a stream. [short pause] The heavens opened; I beheld an apparition of the Heavenly Father in the figure in which He is usually depicted, and, in a voice of thunder, I heard the words: [pause] [solemn, resonant] "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased." [long pause] [hushed, reverent] Jesus was perfectly transparent, entirely penetrated by light; one could scarcely look at Him. [short pause] I saw angels around Him.
