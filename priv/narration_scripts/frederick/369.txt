[somber, measured] The procession comes in sight; the tall horse of the centurion shows first, and leads the way. The trumpet sounds with a wailing clangour. The women look from the lattices above. She sees the thieves, the crosses, everything,—and yet only one thing, Himself. [short pause] [softly] As He draws nigh, the peace of her heart grows deeper. It could not help it; God was approaching, and peace went before Him. [long pause] [deep, sorrowful] Never had maternal love sat on such a throne as that one in Mary’s heart. The anguish was unutterable. God, who knows the number of the sands of the sea, knows it. [short pause] [slow, intense] Now Jesus has come up to her. He halts for a moment. He lifts the one hand that is free, and clears the blood from His eyes. [short pause] [tender] Is it to see her? Rather, that she may see Him, His look of sadness, His look of love. [short pause] She approaches to embrace Him. The soldiers thrust her rudely back. [pained] O misery! and she is His Mother too! [pause] [tender, heartfelt] For a moment she reeled with the push, and then again was still, her eyes fixed on His, His eyes fixed on hers, such a link, such an embrace, such an outpouring of love, such an overflow of sorrow! [long pause] [urgent, grieved] Has He less strength than she? See! He staggers, is overweighed by the burden of the ponderous Cross, and falls with a dull dead sound upon the street, like the clank of falling wood. She sees it. [short pause] [anguished, low] The God of heaven and earth is down. [short pause] Men surround Him, like butchers round a fallen beast; they kick Him, beat Him, swear horrible oaths at Him, drag Him up again with cruel ferocity. [pause] [slow, heavy] It is His third fall. [long pause] [tender, broken] She sees it. He is her Babe of Bethlehem. She is helpless. She cannot get near. Omnipotence held her heart fast. [pause] [softly, steady] In a peace far beyond man’s understanding, she followed slowly on to Calvary, Magdalen and John beside themselves with grief, but feeling as if grace went out from her blue mantle enabling them also to live with broken hearts.
