[calm, narrative] First: Christ our Lord, of the age of twelve years, went up from Nazareth to Jerusalem. [long pause] [quiet, concerned] Second: Christ our Lord remained in Jerusalem, [short pause] and His parents did not know it. [long pause] [measured, then gentle] Third: The three days passed, they found Him disputing in the Temple, and seated in the midst of the doctors; [short pause] and His parents asking Him where He had been, He answered: [pause] [softly, with quiet authority] "'Did you not know that it behooves Me to be in the things which are My Father's?'"
