[solemn, measured] There is other music now for Mary’s ear, the terrible music of dark prophecy which the Holy Ghost utters from His sanctuary in the old priest’s heart; and we would fain think that Simeon held Jesus in his arms when he uttered it, by the very way in which he begins, [short pause] [grave, prophetic] “Behold this Child is set for the fall, and for the resurrection of many in Israel, and for a sign which shall be contradicted. [short pause] [slow, piercing] And thy own soul a sword shall pierce, that out of many hearts thoughts may be revealed.” [long pause] [hushed] Simeon was silent. But over Mary’s soul there came an inexplicable change. [short pause] [thoughtful] Perhaps she learned now what she had not known before. But more probably it only came to her then in another way. [short pause] Yet it was a change, an operation of grace, a new sanctification, an immense work of God. [pause] [sorrowful, measured] A clear and detailed vision of all her sorrows, especially of the whole Passion, was with its minutest circumstances instantaneously impressed upon her soul: and her immaculate heart was deluged with a sea of sorrow, which was supernatural both in its kind and its intensity. [short pause] [softly, tender] It seemed as if the vision came from the very face of Jesus, as if His eyes looked it into her and engraved it there.
