[hushed, awed] The blessed soul of Jesus in dazzling splendor, between two warrior angels and surrounded by a multitude of resplendent figures, came floating down through the rocky roof of the tomb upon the sacred body. [short pause] [softly] It seemed to incline over it and melt, as it were, into one with it. [long pause] [rising wonder] I saw the sacred limbs moving beneath the swathing bands, and the dazzling, living body of the Lord with His soul and His Divinity coming forth from the side of the winding sheet, as if from the wounded Side. [short pause] [quietly, thoughtful] The sight reminded me of Eve coming forth from Adam's side. [pause] [joyful, reverent] The whole place was resplendent with light and glory.
