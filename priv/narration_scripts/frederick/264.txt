[reverent, measured] First: They bring the Child Jesus to the Temple, that He may be presented to the Lord as first-born; [short pause] and they offer for Him "a pair of turtle doves, or two young pigeons." [long pause] [tender, heartfelt] Second: Simeon coming to the Temple "took Him into his arms," saying: [short pause] [softly, with peace] "'Now Thou dost dismiss Thy servant, O Lord, in peace!'" [long pause] [warm, gentle] Third: Anna "coming afterwards confessed to the Lord, and spoke of Him to all that were hoping for the redemption of Israel."
