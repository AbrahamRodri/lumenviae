[warm, measured] All love tends to become like that which it loves. [short pause] God loved man; therefore, He became man. [short pause] Thanks to His human nature, He could take on our woes and our sorrows, and feel the effects of sin as if they were His very own. [long pause] [tender, thoughtful] But all this was conditioned upon Mary’s giving Him a human nature. Without her He would never have had eyes to see the multitude hungry in the desert, or ears to hear the pleading of the lame man of Jericho, or hands to caress children, or feet to seek the lost sheep. [short pause] [hushed, reverent] For nine months, her own body was the natural Eucharist, in which God shared communion with human life, thus preparing for that greater Eucharist, when human life would commune with the Divine. [long pause] [gentle, earnest] Mary’s joy was to form Christ in her own body; her joy now is to form Christ in our souls. [short pause] [prayerful] In this Mystery, we pray to become pregnant with the Christ spirit, giving Him new lips with which He may speak of His Father, new hands with which He may feed the poor, and a new heart with which He may love everyone, even enemies.
