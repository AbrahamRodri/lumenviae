[measured] Jesus taught of the coming of the Kingdom and the fulfillment of the Prophecies. Asking for the Book of Isaias, He unrolled it and read as follows: [pause] [solemn, clear] "The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because the Lord hath anointed Me: He hath sent me to preach to the meek, to heal the contrite of heart, and to preach a release to the captives, [short pause] and deliverance to them that are shut up." [long pause] [warm, earnest] The manner in which Jesus read this text gave His hearers to understand that it was spoken of Himself; that the Spirit of God had descended upon Himself; that He Himself had come to announce salvation to poor, suffering humanity; that all wrong should be made right, widows should be consoled, the sick cured, sinners forgiven. [short pause] [softly, with joy] His words were so beautiful, so loving that, wondering and full of joy, they said one to another: [pause] [hushed, amazed] "He speaks as if He Himself were the Messiah!"
