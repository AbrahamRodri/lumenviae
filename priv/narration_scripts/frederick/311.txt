[solemn, measured] With the servants the Lord, with the criminals the Judge, cometh to be baptized. [short pause] [gentle, reassuring] But be not thou troubled; for in these humiliations His exaltation doth most shine forth. [long pause] [reverent, building] For He who vouchsafed to be borne so long in a Virgin's womb, and to come forth thence with our nature, and to be smitten with rods, and crucified, and to suffer all the rest which He suffered; [short pause] why marvellest thou if He vouchsafed also to be baptized, and to come with the rest to His servant. [pause] [quiet wonder] For the amazement lay in that one thing, that being God, He would be made Man; [short pause] but the rest after this all follows in course of reason.
