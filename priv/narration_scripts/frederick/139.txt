[calm, reverent] Let us contemplate in this mystery, [short pause] how the Blessed Virgin Mary, on the day of her purification, presented the Child Jesus in the Temple, where holy Simeon, giving thanks to God, with great devotion received him into his arms. [long pause] [gently, with devotion] O Holy Virgin, most admirable mistress and pattern of obedience, who didst present the Lord of the Temple, in the Temple of God, obtain for us, of thy blessed Son, that, with holy Simeon and devout Anna, we may praise and glorify him forever. [short pause] [quietly] Amen.
