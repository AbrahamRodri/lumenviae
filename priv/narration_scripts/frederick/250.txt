[hushed, reverent] A pathway of light arose from Mary up to the heavenly Jerusalem, up to the throne of the Most Holy Trinity. On either side of this pathway I saw clouds of light, out of which gazed angelic faces. [short pause] [softly] Mary raised her arms to the Heavenly Jerusalem. Her body with all its wrappings was floating so high above the couch that I could see under it. [short pause] [awed] A figure of light, also with upraised arms, appeared to issue from Mary. The two choirs of angels united under this figure and soared up with it, as if separating it from the body, [quietly] which now sank back upon the couch, the hands crossed upon the breast. [long pause] [warm, joyful] Many holy souls, among whom I recognized Joseph, Anne, Joachim, John the Baptist, Zachary, and Elizabeth, came to meet her. [short pause] [rising, triumphant] But up she soared, followed by them, to her Son, whose Wounds were flashing light far more brilliant than that which surrounded Him. [pause] [tender, solemn] He received her, and placed in her hand a scepter, pointing at the same time over the whole circumference of the earth. [long pause] [joyful, heartfelt] At last I saw, and the sight filled me with joy, a multitude of souls released from Purgatory and soaring up to Heaven; [short pause] [warm, consoling] and I received the surety that every year, on the feast of Mary's Assumption, many of her devout clients are freed from Purgatory.
