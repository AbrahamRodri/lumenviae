[earnest, measured] Wherefore it is necessary to understand the marvel of the Mysteries, what it is, why it was given, and what is the profit of the action. [short pause] We become one Body, and "members of His flesh and of His bones." [short pause] Let the initiated follow what I say. [pause] [tender, heartfelt] In order then that we may become this not by love only, but in very deed, let us be blended into that flesh. This is effected by the food which He hath freely given us, desiring to show the love which He hath for us. [short pause] On this account He hath mixed up Himself with us; He hath kneaded up His body with ours, that we might be a certain One Thing, like a body joined to a head. [long pause] [warm, reflective] For this belongs to them who love strongly; this, for instance, Job implied, speaking of his servants, by whom he was beloved so exceedingly, that they desired to cleave unto his flesh. For they said, to show the strong love which they felt, "Who would give us to be satisfied with his flesh?" [pause] [tender, with wonder] Wherefore this also Christ hath done, to lead us to a closer friendship, and to show His love for us; He hath given to those who desire Him not only to see Him, but even to touch, and eat Him, and fix their teeth in His flesh, and to embrace Him, and satisfy all their love. [long pause] [strong, stirring] Let us then return from that table like lions breathing fire, having become terrible to the devil; [short pause] [softer] thinking on our Head, and on the love which He hath shown for us. [pause] [tender, heartfelt] Parents often entrust their offspring to others to feed; "but I," saith He, "do not so, I feed you with Mine own flesh, desiring that you all be nobly born, and holding forth to you good hopes for the future. [short pause] For He who giveth out Himself to you here, much more will do so hereafter. [short pause] I have willed to become your Brother, for your sake I shared in flesh and blood, and in turn I give out to you the flesh and the blood by which I became your kinsman."
