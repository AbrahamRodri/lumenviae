[reverent, warm] Nothing then is more blessed than the apostles, and especially the three, who even in the cloud were counted worthy to be under the same roof with the Lord. [long pause] [hopeful, rising] But if we will, we also shall behold Christ, not as they then on the mount, but in far greater brightness. [short pause] For not thus shall He come hereafter. [pause] [awed, building slowly] For whereas then, to spare His disciples, He discovered so much only of His brightness as they were able to bear; hereafter He shall come in the very glory of the Father, not with Moses and Elias only, but with the infinite host of the angels, with the archangels, with the cherubim, with those infinite tribes, [short pause] not having a cloud over His head, but even heaven itself being folded up.
