[calm, uplifted] Let us contemplate in this mystery, [short pause] how Our Lord Jesus Christ, forty days after his resurrection, ascended into heaven, attended by angels, in the sight and to the great admiration of his most holy Mother, and his holy Apostles and disciples. [long pause] [warm, with devotion] O Mother of God, Comfortress of the afflicted, as thy beloved Son, when he ascended into heaven, lifted up his hands and blessed his Apostles as he parted from them, so vouchsafe, most holy Mother, to lift up thy pure hands to him in our behalf, that we may enjoy the benefits of his blessing and of thine, here on earth and hereafter in heaven. [short pause] [quietly] Amen.
