[gentle, reflective] Peter desired three tabernacles to be made, one for Moses, one for Elias, and one for Christ. The solitude of the mountain had charms for him; he had been wearied with the tumult of the world's business. [short pause] But why sought he three tabernacles, but because he knew not as yet the unity of the Law, and of Prophecy, and of the Gospel? [long pause] [warm, with wonder] Lastly, he was corrected by the cloud, "While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them." [short pause] Lo, the cloud hath made one tabernacle; wherefore didst thou seek for three? [pause] [softly, with reverence] "And a voice came out of the cloud, This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased, [short pause] hear ye Him."
