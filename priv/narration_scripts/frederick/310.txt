[earnest, measured] Believers know the body of Christ, if they neglect not to be the body of Christ. Let them become the body of Christ, if they wish to live by the Spirit of Christ. [short pause] None lives by the Spirit of Christ but the body of Christ. [long pause] [warm, teaching] Understand, my brethren, what I mean to say. Thou art a man; thou hast both a spirit and a body. I call that a spirit which is called the soul; that whereby it consists that thou art a man, for thou consistest of soul and body. And so thou hast an invisible spirit and a visible body. [short pause] Tell me which lives of the other: does thy spirit live of thy body, or thy body of thy spirit? Every man that lives can answer; and he that cannot answer this, I know not whether he lives: what doth every man that lives answer? [short pause] My body, of course, lives by my spirit. [pause] Wouldst thou then also live by the Spirit of Christ. Be in the body of Christ. For surely my body does not live by thy spirit. My body lives by my spirit, and thy body by thy spirit. [short pause] The body of Christ cannot live but by the Spirit of Christ. [long pause] [rising, fervent] It is for this that the Apostle Paul, expounding this bread, says: "One bread," saith he, "we being many are one body." [pause] O mystery of piety! [short pause] O sign of unity! [short pause] O bond of charity! [pause] [tender, inviting] He that would live has where to live, has whence to live. Let him draw near, let him believe; let him be embodied, that he may be made to live. Let him not shrink from the compact of members; let him not be a rotten member that deserves to be cut off; let him not be a deformed member whereof to be ashamed; let him be a fair, fit, and sound member; let him cleave to the body, live for God by God: [short pause] [softly, with hope] now let him labor on earth, that hereafter he may reign in heaven.
