# Rejected / noted (joyful, luminous)

- Durer Life of the Virgin 08/09/12/15 (Commons "Durer - Life of the Virgin NN.jpg"): only 650px wide; the NGA CC0 scans are far larger, so used those.
- Cleveland Museum of Art Life of the Virgin scans (CC0): valid alternatives, not needed.
- Durer "Presentation of the Virgin in the Temple" (NGA 6696): wrong scene (Mary as a child, not Christ).
- Durer "Birth of the Virgin" (NGA 6695): wrong scene.
- Doré Last Supper and Transfiguration (Commons) are small (about 475-497px wide); no larger Commons copy of either was found, so they are kept at native size, not upscaled.
- Doré Sermon on the Mount: the Commons scan has a printed caption "SERMON ON THE MOUNT" below the plate; cropped off.
- Doré Cana: Christ is small in the composition (a wide banquet scene); it was the only Doré Cana on Commons.
- Schnorr von Carolsfeld alternatives were searched; none found on Commons for these scenes.
- Durer Life of the Virgin scans are cream paper; converted to grayscale. Final Durer files are about 850px wide, not 1600, to stay under ~450KB.
- The Doré provenance on Commons shows "Public domain" (PD-old-type tag) with no more specific licence name in the API extmetadata.
