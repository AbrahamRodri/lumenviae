# Rejected candidates (sorrows-marian)

- Albrecht Durer, "Maria als Schmerzensmutter" (Alte Pinakothek) and "The Virgin as Mother of Sorrows" (c. 1495-1498): painted panels from the Seven Sorrows altarpiece, not woodcuts or engravings; do not match the plates' look.
- Durer, Small Passion "Christ Carrying the Cross" (NGA 6771): Christ has fallen but Mary is not in the scene, so it does not show the meeting with his mother. The Large Passion Bearing of the Cross (already in use as sorrowful_4) shows Mary and the women and is reused for seven_sorrows_4.
- Durer, Life of the Virgin "Christ Taking Leave of His Mother" (NGA 6709): the parting at home, not the meeting on the Way of the Cross.
- Durer, Large Passion Deposition and Lamentation (MET DP816614, DP816610): the Small Passion Lamentation already in the folder covers seven_sorrows_6 in the same series as the new Entombment.
- Durer, "Virgin and Child on a Crescent with a Diadem" (NGA 6677, 1514 engraving): near duplicate of the Madonna on the Crescent already in the folder.
- Durer, "Virgin and Child with the Pear" (NGA 42403): held back; the monkey and dragonfly engravings already cover the engraved Madonna group.
- Small Passion Entombment from Wikimedia Commons "Dürer - Small Passion 28.jpg": only 423x550 px.
