# Rejected or not found (sorrowful, glorious)

- glorious_4 (Assumption): NOT COVERED. Durer's Life of the Virgin has no separate Assumption plate; Assumption and Coronation is one print (B.94), already in the repo as coronation-durer.jpg. Searches of Commons for other PD woodcut Assumptions (Schongauer, Schnorr, Cranach, Baldung, Heller altarpiece drawing) found nothing usable. Reusing the same print would duplicate a file.
- Christ on the Mount of Olives MET DP816594 (Large Passion): alternate impression of the used plate; DP816593 chosen because it carries a date.
- Christ Carrying the Cross MET DP820458 (Large Passion): alternate impression of the same plate as DP816602; not needed.
- Ecce Homo MET DP816601 (Large Passion): a good plate but depicts the presentation to the people, not the crowning; not used for sorrowful_3. Available as a general plate.
- Pentecost MET DP816003 and Ascension MET DP816001 (Small Passion): second impressions; downloads were rate limited and the first impressions were sufficient.
- Crowning with Thorns is not in the Large Passion, so sorrowful_3 is from the Small Passion (same as the existing crucifixion plate and the Ascension and Pentecost). Sorrowful 1, 2, 4 are Large Passion (same as the existing resurrection plate).
- Files are 1300-1500px long side at JPEG quality 50 to stay near 450KB (the MET scans are heavy line work).
