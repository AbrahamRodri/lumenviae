## Notes

- A file may serve more than one key when one print shows both scenes: the Seven Sorrows reuse the Presentation (1), the Finding in the Temple (3), the Bearing of the Cross (4) and the Crucifixion (5).
- glorious_4 is the Death of the Virgin from the Life of the Virgin: the series has no separate Assumption, whose print is shared with the Coronation (glorious_5).
- The Transfiguration and Last Supper come from The Bible Panorama (1891), scanned by the Library of Congress and shared through Internet Archive Book Images on Flickr Commons, whose Commons tag reads "No restrictions". They are public domain by date everywhere: Dore died in 1883 and the book was published in 1891. Recorded as PD-old-100.
- Where two fragments gave the same key, existing.json wins, then fragments in file-name order.
